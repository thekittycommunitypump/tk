Frequently Asked Questions

What is TKCP?

TKCP — The Kitty Community Pump — is a community-driven meme coin project focused on memes, creativity, participation and community growth.

Where is the contract address?

TKCP is planned for Solana. The official mint address will be published here after deployment and verification. Never use an address copied from an unofficial source.

Will the website show live trading data?

Yes. After the official Solana mint address and trading pair are known, the dashboard can connect to an appropriate blockchain/DEX data source for live price, volume, liquidity and trading activity.

Is the TKCP supply fixed?

TKCP is configured for a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens with 18 decimals. The mint authority is intended to be permanently renounced, meaning no additional tokens can be minted once that authority is revoked on-chain.

No statement on this website should be treated as financial or investment advice. Crypto assets are risky and visitors should do their own research.
